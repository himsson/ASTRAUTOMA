# Code of Conduct

ASTRAUTOMA is a place for people who like rockets and want them to fly well.

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to KSP or to programming.
- Criticize code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind.
- Keep discussions on topic and in English or Russian.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments and block people who break these rules.
To report a problem, contact [himsson](https://github.com/himsson) privately.

Based on the ideas of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
